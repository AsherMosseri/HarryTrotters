# 3.0 change list (draft, for the update log at launch)

Running list of what's different in the redesign (branch `redesign`, tested at /next/).

## New look (iPad and computer)
- Sidebar in place of the row of tabs: pages in groups, chat channels and messages right in it, your menu at the bottom.
- New Today page (the app opens on it): your tasks, what's due this week, who's at the lab, unread chat, next milestone, competition countdown.
- Tasks is one page with a Board / Table switch and a big title; cards show one line of details instead of badges.
- Chat fills the page beside the sidebar; new channel, channel settings, and new message from the sidebar.
- Line icons instead of emoji in chat, the message box, the shift button, and Filters.
- Pages (Shifts, Parts, Milestones, Match log, Checklists, Roster, Resources, Robot): big page titles, stat boxes become one row of plain numbers, boxed panels become plain sections, toggles become one quiet control, emoji removed from buttons.
- Roster: people are a list (one row each: subteams, responsibilities, open tasks) instead of a grid of cards.
- Milestones page is titled Milestones (the countdown line stays under it).
- Empty chats say "Nothing in #channel yet." instead of a big welcome.
- Fewer explainer lines under page titles.
- Pop-ups (task details, new task, menus, notifications, Help): plain labels instead of small capitals, soft picker buttons, quieter Delete, line icons in notifications, no emoji in the theme menu.
- Help rewritten for the new layout (Today, the sidebar, where your menu is) with the emoji and icon tiles taken out.
- Parts: the #parts chat beside the list can be hidden (Hide in the chat, Show #parts to bring it back, with the unread count). Remembered on each device.
