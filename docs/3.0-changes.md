# 3.0 change list (draft, for the update log at launch)

Running list of what's different in the redesign (branch `redesign`, tested at /next/).

## New look (iPad and computer)
- Sidebar in place of the row of tabs: pages in groups, chat channels and messages right in it, your menu at the bottom.
- New Today page (the app opens on it): your tasks, what's due this week, who's at the lab, unread chat, next milestone, competition countdown.
- Tasks is one page with a Board / Table switch and a big title; cards show one line of details instead of badges.
- Chat fills the page beside the sidebar; new channel, channel settings, and new message from the sidebar.
- Line icons instead of emoji in chat, the message box, the shift button, and Filters.
- Pages (Shifts, Parts, Milestones, Match log, Checklists, Roster, Resources, Robot): big page titles, stat boxes become one row of plain numbers, boxed panels become plain sections, toggles become one quiet control, emoji removed from buttons.
- Roster: people are a list (one row each: subteams, responsibilities, open tasks) instead of a grid of cards.
- Milestones page is titled Milestones (the countdown line stays under it).
- Empty chats say "Nothing in #channel yet." instead of a big welcome.
- Fewer explainer lines under page titles.
- Pop-ups (task details, new task, menus, notifications, Help): plain labels instead of small capitals, soft picker buttons, quieter Delete, line icons in notifications, no emoji in the theme menu.
- Help rewritten for the new layout (Today, the sidebar, where your menu is) with the emoji and icon tiles taken out.
- Parts: the #parts chat beside the list can be hidden (Hide in the chat, Show #parts to bring it back, with the unread count). Remembered on each device.
- Phones: the same new pages, a big page title at the top (with + for a new task on Tasks), and a tab bar of Today, Tasks, Chat, Shifts, More with line icons. More is a plain list. The floating + button is gone. Today is always the first tab.
- Board on phones: statuses are a row of words with the picked one underlined, instead of pills.
- Chat: the message toolbar uses line icons (reply, pin, make a task, edit, remove reactions, delete); replies show a small arrow and name instead of a gray bar; reactions and task links are quieter; a message that mentions you gets a light accent tint; Pinned shows on pinned messages; link previews are plain cards.
- Chat panels: "Online · 4" instead of an em dash, no emoji in the pins/search titles, and announcements, muted channels, and group messages use line icons.
- Chat notes (editing, uploading, read-only channels) lost their emoji.
- Full-screen moments (sign in, the update screen, lab check-in, required polls, the shutdown screen): no more colored logo squares; a small label, a big plain title, and left-aligned text. The welcome card, lab log, and shutdown screen lost their emoji ("Welcome, Asher", "From Asher").
- Toasts: "Shift started", "Shift ended.", "Logged. Your shift is over." instead of emoji and "See you next time!".
- Emoji taken out of everyday labels: Controls switches, feature requests, the version log, the electrical diagram, robot pins, poll and standup forms, the team bot cards (/task, /standup, /shift who, /overdue), and file attachments (they show the file type instead). Games, the casino, and profile decorations keep theirs.
- The sidebar can fold to a strip of icons (the button next to the bell; tap it again to unfold). Handy on an iPad held upright. Each device remembers it.
- Your Controls, Commands, and Ads pages: no emoji on the buttons and plain section headings; command groups lose their emoji (also in /help).
- The Team page is called Roster everywhere (sidebar, More, Edit tabs, Controls).
- Your menu is shorter: your name (tap for your profile), Edit profile, Jackson coins, Request a feature, Appearance, Help (and Controls for Asher), and Switch user. Light/dark, color themes, and Calm chat moved into Appearance; Ads moved into Controls; Edit tabs is only on phones; the update check is in Help.
- The sidebar's fold button sits next to your name at the bottom.
- Sidebar top: the team name always fits; while you're on a shift, the timer is its own "On shift" row under the name, and its pop-up opens onto the page instead of off the edge.
- Small pictures (the sidebar's messages list, 24px avatars) show people's avatar decorations and stickers, so they match their profile.
- Board: it fits the screen again; a long column (like Done) scrolls inside itself instead of stretching the whole page.
- Profile cards: a thick decoration ring around someone's picture stays inside the card instead of getting cut off at the edge.
- Roster: tapping someone's name or picture opens their profile (in the People list, and at the top of the edit window; teammates' taps on the org chart do too).
- Starting up: a big centered Harry Trotters splash with a moving bar (shown the moment the app opens, before anything loads), instead of small "Loading…" text.
- Chat header: the picture or icon, the name, and the topic sit on one centered line (DMs and announcements were misaligned).
- Jackson coins: the casino server now counts coins from finished tasks (it was skipping all of them, so it thought everyone had less than the app showed).
- Jackson coins: lab hours, the start date, and "today" for prizes use the team's day (New York) on every phone and on the server.
- Jackson coins: the casino reads your spending and gifts fresh for every bet (it used numbers up to 20 seconds old).
- Jackson coins: shifts, gifts, purchases, and casino winnings follow someone after a rename.
- Jackson coins: time on a running shift keeps adding to your balance while the app is open.
- Jackson coins: gifts and ad prizes are paid by the server, which checks your balance and today's limits (they could be faked from a browser before, and changing the phone's date gave extra wheel spins).
- Jackson coins: a purchase, gift, or prize that doesn't go through says so instead of showing success; double taps can't pay twice.
- Jackson coins: /give takes a whole number and asks which person when a name fits more than one.
- Jackson coins: with coins switched off, the prize wheel, ad games, skips, and Remove ads are hidden.
- Jackson coins: a connection error no longer empties your spending history for the rest of the session.
- Today: Start a shift works (the button was sending the tap itself as shift details, so it failed).
- Starting up: the app remembers that this device is on the team and opens right away; the check with the server runs in the background (it used to hold everyone on the splash until it answered).
- Starting up: the splash's bar fills as each step finishes, names the step, and after 10 seconds says it's slow and offers Reload.
- Games: a rock paper scissors where someone never picks settles itself (whoever picked wins; nobody picked, bets back) instead of keeping both bets forever.
- Games: a tic-tac-toe or connect four left alone for 30 minutes ends (whoever's turn it was loses); blackjack, mines, and high-low left alone for 30 minutes are played out (stand, or cash out, or the bet back if nothing was played).
- Games: the server finishes games every 10 minutes even if nobody has the card open, so bets don't sit stuck.
- Games: trivia, hangman, and type races pay up to 100 coins a day each person (they could be farmed for about 20 coins a minute).
- /daily in demo mode uses the team's day, like the real one.
- Shift hours: one way of adding them up everywhere (Shifts page, profiles, /whois, the lab log, Today, coins); shifts that overlap for one person (two devices, or a manager adding one over another) count once instead of twice; profiles count shifts from before a rename.
- Shift hours: shown with one decimal everywhere (the Hours list rounded anything over 10 to a whole number, so it didn't match the tiles).
- Shifts: "Add a missed shift" needs an end time (without one it was saved as still running and kept counting); lengths over 16 hours, ends before starts, and future starts are flagged.
- Shifts: picking a start time a few minutes ahead in "Started earlier?" means now (it used to mean yesterday, making a 24-hour shift).
- The team logo's surprise is our robot (a cut-out photo) dancing: it strafes side to side, turns in place, and bops to the beat. It used to be a cartoon cat.
- Your menu: closing anything you opened from it (✕, Escape, Cancel, or Save) brings you back to the menu, on iPad and phone; opening Edit from your profile card and closing it goes back to the card, then the menu.
- Profile card and full profile: the status bubble sits beside the top of the head, its trail pointing at the head (it pointed at the online dot).
