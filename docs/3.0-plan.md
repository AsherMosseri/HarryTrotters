## 0. Getting the new app ☑

When teammates open their old Home Screen app (added before 3.0) on an
iPhone or iPad, it shows **Get the new app**: open the website in Safari, add
it to the Home Screen (the new icon is shown), then delete the old app (shown
with its old icon). iOS never updates an added app's icon, and the new app is
where everyone sets up their password. The new app is recognized because it
opens at `?home=3` (the Safari page and the manifest both point there).
Preview it on /next/ with `?demo=newapp`.

## C. Lab log, rebuilt

The goal: as little typing as possible, no drop-downs, big buttons, and only
the first step required.

| # | Step | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| C1 ☑ | **Smart skip** | If the shift was under 15 minutes, show one button: "Quick visit, nothing to log". |
| C2 ☑ | **1. What did you work on?** *(required)* | Big multi-select buttons: General, Coding, Mechanical, CAD, Electrical, Assembly, Outreach. Your own subteams come first and are already selected. (Coding = the Programming subteam. Outreach isn't a subteam, so it's a lab log category only.) |
| C3 ☑ | **2. Which task?** | For each area you picked: your open tasks there as buttons, then the team's other open tasks, then "Something else". Tasks you touched today (opened, moved or commented on) are already selected. |
| C4 ☑ | **3. How did it go?** | One tap per task: Finished, Made progress, Stuck, Just started. "Finished" offers to move the task to Done. "Stuck" asks one short question: "What's blocking it?" (and marks the task Blocked if you want). |
| C5 ☑ | **4. One line** *(optional)* | One short box, "Anything worth noting?", with suggestion chips under it for the areas you picked. Tapping a chip fills in the text, which you can edit. The chips learn from what the team taps most, so common ones move to the front. |
| C6 ☑ | **Chips** | Coding: Tuned auto, Fixed a bug, Tested on the robot, Worked on TeleOp, Vision / Limelight. Mechanical: Built, Took apart / rebuilt, Fixed something broken, Measured / tested. CAD: Modeled a part, Updated the assembly, Made a drawing, Exported for printing. Electrical: Wired, Cable managed, Fixed a connection, Tested motors / sensors. General: Cleaned up, Helped someone, Practiced driving, Meeting, Outreach. (Assembly and Outreach get their own too.) |
| C7 ☑ | **Same as last time** | One button fills in your previous log; you change what's different. |
| C8 ☑ | **Photo** | One tap to the camera, attached to the entry. |
| C9 ☑ | **Voice note** | Hold to record up to 30 seconds instead of typing; the team can play it. |
| C10 ☑ | **Worked with** | Tap the faces of teammates who were at the lab today. Their logs then suggest the same tasks. |
| C11 ☑ | **Done is always visible** | Every step after the first can be skipped with one Done button, always on screen. |
| C12 ☑ | **Payoff after you submit** | "You've logged 12 h on Coding this month", "3 tasks moved forward today", your streak. |
| C13 ☑ | **#lab-log as one card a day** | Instead of one message per person, a single card per day that fills in as people log. |
| C14 ☑ | **Portfolio-ready** | Entries are structured (area, task, how it went, photo), so later they can be exported as engineering notebook pages. 3.0 stores them in that shape; the export comes later. |
| — | **Avoid** | Required extra steps, asking how long you worked (the shift knows), and free-text boxes before the buttons. |

## G. Educational games

**Question banks: done** (`functions/banks/`, server only; validator:
`node functions/banks/validate.js`). Rules 523 and strategy 104 (from the
2026-27 manual, each with rule and page), code 238 (189 Java + 49 Blocks),
parts 202 parts → 447 questions, spot the problem 67, wiring 90 (matches the
app's hub model), gear ratios generated (572 distinct). Daily question: 365
days, all different. **Missing:** part photos — this environment's network
blocks gobilda.com and revrobotics.com; each part has its product page link,
and photo questions turn on as soon as photos are added. The game screens
(G1–G11 UI) are still to build.

Rules for all of them: a large question bank, nothing repeats until you've
seen the whole bank, every question checked for accuracy, and a real,
polished look. **Answers live on the server**, not in the app: otherwise
anyone could read them out of the app's code and farm coins.

| # | Game | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| G1 ☐ | **Name that part** | A part picture and four answer buttons. Hundreds of parts, with product photos from the goBILDA and REV websites. |
| G2 ☐ | **Rules check** | A short game-manual scenario, true/false or multiple choice. From the 2026-27 Competition Manual (BIOBUZZ); every question cites its rule number. |
| G3 ☐ | **Wiring puzzle** | Connect a motor, encoder or sensor to the right port on a hub picture, using the electrical diagram's hub drawings and port rules. |
| G4 ☐ | **Spot the problem** | A photo of a build or wiring job with one thing wrong; tap the mistake. Needs real photos of builds with a mistake (team photos, plus product photos where they fit). |
| G5 ☐ | **Fix the bug** | A few lines of robot code (Java, plus Blocks-style) with one mistake; tap the broken line. Easy to write lots of these accurately. |
| G6 ☐ | **Gear ratio challenge** | "312 RPM through a 2:1 reduction: wheel speed?" Generated from real goBILDA/REV motor speeds and wheel sizes, so it never runs out. |
| G7 ☐ | **Auto path planner** | Drag a path on a field picture to score as much as possible in 30 seconds. Needs this season's field and scoring. |
| G8 ☐ | **Who did this?** | A recent lab log entry or finished task; guess who did it. Built from the team's own data, so it doesn't repeat and stays fresh. |
| G9 ☐ | **Robot timeline** | Put robot versions and milestones in order, from the team's data. |
| G10 ☐ | **Daily question** | One question a day in chat, drawn from all the banks above. Coins for the first correct answers, plus a daily-answer streak. |
| G11 ☐ | **Field predictions** | Guess the team's score before a practice run or match; closest guess wins coins. Guesses lock when the run starts. |

## Decisions

Answers go here as they come in.

- **Shipping:** hold 3.0 until every item is done; phases are the build order.
- **Rules source:** the 2026-27 Competition Manual (BIOBUZZ, version TU07,
  171 pages), uploaded by Asher. Every rules question cites its rule number.
- **Part pictures:** product photos from the goBILDA and REV websites (Asher's
  call: the app is private to the team).
- **Coin rate:** 4 coins an hour (1 per 15 minutes, paid in 15-minute steps).
  Daily cap (10 h) and 15 coins per finished task unchanged.
- **Market (F1):** sells pet items, real perks, chat extras and game entries.
  Real perks are added only by team management; the app suggests ideas for
  them to pick from. No pinned-message item.
- **Pins are broken:** fix pinning messages in chat (added as A4).
- **Pets (H3):** everyone can adopt their own pet (optional, you can have
  none); plus a team mascot: the robot.
- **Match schedule (D1):** both: pulled from the FTC Events API when it works,
  typed in at the event when it doesn't. Asher added the key as GitHub secrets
  `FTC_API_USER` and `FTC_API_KEY` (Oct 31); the deploy loads them into the server.
- **Weekly recap (E1):** Sundays at 6 PM; top contributor by a balanced score
  (hours, tasks finished, lab log entries).
- **Shift edits (B1):** anyone can delete their own shifts or make them
  shorter; making one longer, or adding one, goes to team management to
  approve first.
- **Voice notes (C9):** yes, up to 30 seconds, playable by the team.
- **2-player Tetris / Minesweeper (H1):** a live race on the same pieces or
  board, the other person's progress shown live.
- **More animation (A3):** all four areas (celebrations, moving things, page
  changes, buttons and taps), each with its own on/off switch in Appearance.
- **Daily streak (B4):** only school days count, from the 2026-27 American
  Heritage calendar (`functions/shared/school.js`). Days off for only some
  grades (PSAT/SAT) count as days off, so nobody's streak breaks on them.
- **Lab check-in (new, B5):** a schedule option "every school day", asked at
  12:00 AM that day (school days from the same schedule).
